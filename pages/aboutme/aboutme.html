Born in Greenland in 1978 aboard the coastal passenger ship M/S KununguaK—the vessel that gave me my first name.

After high school and a year on an art school scholarship, I worked a wide range of jobs: waiter, bartender, unskilled laborer, airport security, runway sealer, illustrator, and political cartoonist for a national newspaper.

I later moved to Denmark, working in hospitality, education, and B2B sales before mandatory military service set me on a new path, with deployments to Iraq, Kosovo, and Afghanistan.

In 2010, I began my journey into digital work through studies in multimedia design, where I discovered a lasting interest in UX research, UI design, coding, and 3D modeling. I later pursued a Bachelor’s degree in Innovation and Entrepreneurship, prioritizing hands-on learning and launching my own venture, Taskbuddy IVS.

From 2016 to 2018, I worked as a freelance digital generalist—covering UX, UI, web development, 3D, video, and game design—while living as a digital nomad.

Since 2019, I’ve worked remotely for a single client in the water utility sector, while living in cities including Berlin, Tallinn, Copenhagen, Athens, Barcelona, and Kyiv.

Now back in Barcelona, I’m looking for my next challenge. I enjoy startups, strong teams, and good leadership—and I’m equally comfortable working beyond pure coding, wherever software meets real-world problems.

Off the clock, I enjoy running, hiking, learning Ukulele, Spanish, and meditating.
